I am a PhD student in Civil Engineering (Transportation) and a Graduate Research Assistant at Worcester Polytechnic Institute. I received my B.Sc. in Civil Engineering from Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), Bangladesh, in 2025, advised by Dr. Md. Basir Zisan.

Before my PhD I worked on computer vision for infrastructure, traffic microsimulation with SUMO, and interpretable machine learning for travel behavior. I also co-founded Two Min Civil Engineering, a YouTube channel that explains civil engineering concepts.

WPI – CEAE
Worcester, MA, USA

My research applies natural language processing (NLP), large language models (LLMs) and machine learning to transportation planning and operations.

Currently, I am building LLM-based pipelines that turn what transit riders write into structured, mapped service requests that agencies can act on, and domain-specific language models for transportation feedback that work across cities and platforms.

I am also interested in computer vision for infrastructure monitoring, intelligent transportation systems, and explainable machine learning.
