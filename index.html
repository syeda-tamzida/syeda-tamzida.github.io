About Me

I am a PhD student in Civil Engineering (Transportation) at Worcester Polytechnic Institute. My research applies machine learning, natural language processing (NLP) and large language models (LLMs) to transportation planning and operations. My current work combines LLM-based information extraction with geospatial analysis: I build models that read what riders write to transit agencies and on social media, work out what they are asking for and where, and turn it into data that planners can act on.

Research Interests
  • NLP and LLMs for transportation: text classification, information extraction, NER
  • Domain-specific language models and LoRA fine-tuning
  • LLM agents with tool use and evidence verification
  • Data-driven public transit planning and geospatial analysis
  • Intelligent transportation systems, traffic simulation and reinforcement learning

LLM-Based Extraction of Transit Service Requests from Public Comments

Graduate Research Assistant, WPI · Fall 2026 – ongoing

CS 554 Natural Language Processing, WPI · Fall 2026 – ongoing

  • Cross-platform learning from Reddit and Pittsburgh transit public comments, to tell genuinely new rider concerns apart from changes caused only by platform or wording
  • Working toward a transit-specific language model that works for any city and identifies each request’s type and location

ML-Based Dynamic Exposure Assessment for Urban Hazard Risk

Tomorrow’s Cities · 2026

  • Designing ML models (Random Forest, XGBoost) to predict spatial risk patterns from synthetic exposure data (buildings, population, land use)
  • Incorporating human mobility and time-dependent population to compare dynamic and static exposure in urban risk estimates
  • Testing cross-city generalization across 10 cities and identifying key risk factors with feature-importance methods

Projects

Evidence-Grounded LLM Trading Agent

Course project, WPI · Fall 2026

  • Fine-tuned Qwen 2.5 (1.5B) with LoRA to build a tool-calling agent that researches and checks SEC filings, news and company announcements before each trade
  • Every trade must cite evidence IDs; code verifies the citations and cancels trades the model cannot support, guarding against hallucinated sources
  • BM25 retrieval memory of the agent’s own reflections; deployed for a week of live automated paper trading through the Alpaca API

GitHub

Gemma-3-1B: Prompting, LoRA/QLoRA Fine-Tuning and BM25 Retrieval

CS 554 Natural Language Processing, WPI · Fall 2026

  • Fine-tuned Gemma-3-1B with LoRA and QLoRA on SQuAD v2 to answer with short spans from the passage and abstain (“NOT IN CONTEXT”) when the answer is absent: LoRA raised exact match from 64 to 74 and correct abstention from 24% to 74%
  • Built a BM25 retriever over 12,241 SciQ paragraphs; top-1 retrieval-augmented generation lifted exact match from 0.7 to 80.7, and more passages hurt the small model
  • Compared fine-tuning and retrieval in a 2 × 2 design to separate what changes the model’s behavior from what supplies knowledge

GitHub

BanReady: Snow-Ban Parking Assistant
