My research applies natural language processing (NLP), large language models (LLMs) and machine learning to transportation planning and operations.

Currently, I am building LLM-based pipelines that turn what transit riders write into structured, mapped service requests that agencies can act on, and domain-specific language models for transportation feedback that work across cities and platforms.

I am also interested in computer vision for infrastructure monitoring, intelligent transportation systems, and explainable machine learning.

LLM agent

Evidence-Grounded LLM Trading Agent

Course project, WPI · Fall 2026

Fine-tuned Qwen 2.5 (1.5B) with LoRA to build a tool-using agent that researches SEC filings, news and market indicators before each decision and must cite its evidence. Code verifies every citation and cancels decisions the model cannot support. A BM25 memory retrieves the agent’s own past reflections. Ran for a week of live paper trading.

SUMO · ns-3

V2V Communication Delay in CAV Platoons: SUMO + ns-3 Pipeline and Dataset

Independent · 2025 · Preprint

An end-to-end SUMO → ns-3 co-simulation pipeline: six five-vehicle CAV platoons on a 3×3 grid, IEEE 802.11p V2V communication, and an open dataset of 27,913 received packets. A leakage-free analysis shows the channel saturates within seconds (median delay about 420 ms) and identifies what drives delay.

Statistics

Salary Disclosure and Job-Posting Engagement

DS 502 Statistical Methods for ML, WPI · Fall 2026 · Team project

Analyzed 123,849 LinkedIn job postings with regression (company-clustered standard errors), Ridge and Lasso. Postings that disclose salary receive about 10% more views, and the effect grows with seniority, reaching 36% for executive roles.
